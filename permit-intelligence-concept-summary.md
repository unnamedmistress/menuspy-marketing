# Building Permit Intelligence Service — Concept Summary

## The Winner: **PermitNinja**

### One-Line Pitch
Hyper-local permit intelligence starting in Austin, TX — helping homeowners and contractors determine if they need a permit, what it requires, and how to get it.

### The Problem
Homeowners and small contractors face consistent uncertainty:
- Don't know when a permit is legally required
- Don't want to pull one unless necessary (cost, time, inspections)
- Rules vary dramatically by the 40,000+ US jurisdictions
- Municipal websites are mazes with inconsistent answers
- No existing service solves this end-to-end for non-experts

### The Solution
A webapp that provides:
1. **Permit Diagnostic** — 5-7 questions → YES/NO/MAYBE with confidence score
2. **Requirements Guide** — Forms, fees, timelines, inspection schedule
3. **Documentation Generator** — Pre-filled applications, audit trail PDF
4. **Contractor Bridge** — Connect to vetted local pros who handle permits

### Why It Wins
1. **Hyper-local first** — Perfect in Austin beats mediocre everywhere
2. **Survived all objections** — Defensible, monetizable, buildable
3. **Clear MVP path** — Launch with no-code tools, manual fulfillment
4. **Natural expansion** — Proven model rolls to new cities by demand

### What Killed the Other Concepts

| Concept | Fatal Flaw |
|---------|-----------|
| Nationwide PermitCheckr | Impossible to maintain 40,000 jurisdictions |
| PermitRisk | Quantifying "illegal" activity is liability nightmare |
| Full PermitPath | Integration hell with government systems |
| CityBridge | Browser extensions hard to distribute |
| CityOS (B2G) | 18-month sales cycles to municipalities |

- Contractor relationships create marketplace network effects
- Brand trust from "Austin permit experts" positioning
