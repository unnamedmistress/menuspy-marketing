# Persona Log: The Skeptic

## Initial Impression (Turn 1)

**First thought:** Another "simplify government" app. I've seen these before - they either overpromise, sell your data, or disappear in 6 months.

**Immediate concerns:**
- No privacy policy visible on homepage
- No "About Us" or company information
- No indication of whether this is affiliated with Pinellas County or a third party
- The URL (vercel.app) suggests this is a prototype/personal project, not an official government tool

**Homepage observation:** The "What You'll Need" section says "That's it! We'll tell you everything else." This sounds like marketing speak. What exactly am I committing to by starting?

## Primary Flow Exploration (Turn 2)

**Started a "Water Heater" job:**
- The job type dropdown is comprehensive, but where is this data coming from? Is it actually tied to county requirements or just a generic list?
- The cost estimate ($800-$2,500) and time estimate (3-6 hours) are helpful context, but are these official estimates or crowd-sourced guesses?
- No warning about what happens to my data when I proceed

**What I checked:**
- Looked for Terms of Service - not found
- Looked for Privacy Policy - not found  
- Looked for data retention info - not found
- Checked if there's any government endorsement - none visible

**Red flag:** The "Try AI chat with photos" button appears everywhere. What's happening to those photos? Are they stored? Analyzed by who?

## AI Assistant Exploration (Turn 3)

**Tried the AI assistant:**
- Input field allows free text entry
- Photo upload accepts up to 5 images
- No explanation of what AI model is being used
- No disclosure of where prompts/photos are sent
- "Go to wizard" suggests data flows between modes - is my chat history stored?

**Critical issue:** The permit office dropdown defaults to "Pinellas County (Unincorporated)" but includes cities like St. Petersburg, Clearwater, etc. Missed this and I could submit to the wrong jurisdiction.

**Phone number on help page:** The county number (727-464-3207) is real, which adds credibility, but doesn't mean the app itself is official.

## Final Assessment (Turn 4)

**What's missing for me to trust this:**
1. Privacy Policy explaining data handling
2. Terms of Service clarifying liability
3. Disclosure of AI processing (where do photos go?)
4. Clear statement of relationship to Pinellas County
5. GDPR/CCPA compliance info
6. Data retention and deletion policy

**What could go wrong:**
- Photos of my home uploaded to unknown servers
- Personal address data stored unencrypted
- Checklist items could be wrong, causing permit rejection
- App disappears, taking my job history with it

**Frustrations:**
- Account page is a 404
- No way to delete data or view stored information
- "3-5 minutes" estimate ignores the actual county portal work

**One good thing:** The job type list is impressively comprehensive and categorized logically.

**Verdict:** Suspicious. Would need legal/docs before using for real permits.
