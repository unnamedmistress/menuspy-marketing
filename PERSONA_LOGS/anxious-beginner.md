# Persona Log: The Anxious Beginner

## Initial Impression (Turn 1)

**First thought:** I need to get a permit for a water heater but I have NO idea what I'm doing. This looks friendly, but I'm terrified I'll mess something up that can't be undone.

**Immediate anxieties:**
- "Takes about 3-5 minutes" - what happens after that? Is that the actual permit or just a checklist?
- The "What You'll Need" section says "That's it!" - but what if I don't have my contractor's info yet?
- Can I go back if I make a mistake?
- Is my data being saved or do I lose everything if I close the app?

**Looking for reassurance:**
- ✓ Clear "Before you start" list helps me prepare
- ✓ Friendly language ("We'll help you every step")
- ✗ No "Learn more" links explaining what a permit even is
- ✗ No indication if this costs money
- ✗ No phone number visible for human help

## Primary Flow Exploration (Turn 2)

**Starting the Water Heater job:**
**Anxiety level: 7/10**

**What scared me:**
- The dropdown says "Choose job type" but there are SO many options. What if I pick the wrong one? Is "Water Heater" the same as "Water Heater Replacement"?
- The cost estimate ($800-$2,500) showed up immediately - will I be charged this through the app?
- The time estimate says 3-6 hours - is that the permit approval time or installation time?

**What I was looking for but couldn't find:**
- A "What's the difference between these options?" helper
- Hover tooltip explaining each job type
- Ability to go back and change my selection (Back button was disabled)
- Preview of next steps before committing

**The tabs worry me:**
- Job Type → Location → Details → Requirements
- I'm on step 1 of 4. What happens at step 4? Is that when I submit and can't undo?
- No "save draft" option - if I close my browser, is my progress gone?

**Relief:** The "Try AI chat with photos" alternative is visible. If I get stuck, I can try that instead.

## AI Assistant Exploration (Turn 3)

**Tried the AI chat approach:**
**Anxiety level: 6/10**

**First impressions:**
+ I can type in "simple words" - that feels less scary than official forms
+ I can upload photos to explain ("a picture is worth a thousand words")
+ There's a "Go to wizard" button if I get confused

**What's making me nervous:**
- The address field is pre-filled with a fake/example address. What if I don't notice and click Send? Will it submit to the wrong place?
- The permit office dropdown defaulted to "Pinellas County (Unincorporated)" - I don't know what "unincorporated" means! Is that me?
- The Send button is disabled until I type something - but there's no message saying *why* it's disabled

**Questions I have but can't ask:**
- What if my job doesn't fit any category?
- What if I get something wrong in my description?
- Can the AI correct me or will it just work with what I give it?
- How private is this conversation?
- What happens to my photos after I upload them?

**Comforting elements:**
- The "Chat with AI Get instant answers" button on the help page is inviting
- The county phone number is visible if I panic
- The help section exists (though I haven't clicked it yet)

## Help Section Exploration (Turn 4)

**Finally checking Help:**
**Anxiety level: 4/10**

**What I found helpful:**
+ Common questions are listed! "Do I need an inspection?" is EXACTLY what I was wondering
+ Phone number to county is clearly displayed
+ Hours are listed (Monday-Friday, 8-5)
+ Email support option exists

**What confused me:**
- The FAQ questions have a chevron, suggesting they expand... but when I clicked one, it took me to the AI chat instead of showing an answer here
- Video tutorials have durations (3 min, 2 min, etc.) but I can't tell if they actually exist or are just links
- The "Getting Started" video sounds perfect for me, but I can't tell if it's really there

**I wish:**
- The FAQ actually answered on the page
- There was a glossary explaining terms like "unincorporated"
- A step-by-step guide existed showing what happens after I submit
- I could see a sample completed checklist before starting

## Final Assessment

**What's giving me confidence:**
- Friendly, non-technical language throughout
- Clear "what you'll need" preparation list
- Visual design doesn't look scary/official
- Multiple help/support options visible
- Progress indicators in the wizard

**What's keeping me up at night:**
- Account page is broken (404) - so I can't save my progress
- No indication of when/where I actually pay the county for the permit
- Unfamiliar terminology ("unincorporated") with no explanation
- Can't preview what the final checklist looks like before committing
- Don't know if I can edit after submitting
- No confirmation that this is actually connected to the real county system
- "We'll tell you everything else" feels vague - what if "everything else" includes something I can't provide?

**What I need to proceed:**
1. A "What to expect" guide showing the full process through permitting
2. Explanations for technical terms
3. Ability to save without account (or working account system)
4. Sample checklist preview
5. Clear statement of "You'll still need to submit through the official county portal"

**Would I use it?** I'd probably call the county first to ask if this app is legit, then maybe try the AI chat since it feels less committal than the wizard.

**One critical realization:** This app makes me a CHECKLIST, not the actual permit. The real work happens AFTER. That wasn't completely clear at first.
