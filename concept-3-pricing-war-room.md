# Concept 3: The "Pricing War Room" Pop-Up Experience

## Overview
A **traveling pop-up installation** at major restaurant conferences — a physical "war room" with wall-sized competitive maps.

## Problem Insight
Operators need to *see* the competitive landscape visually. Remote demos lack urgency and memorability.

## Core Solution
Immersive 15-minute experience with wall-sized real-time competitive visualizations.

## Target Users
- Primary: Conference-attending multi-location operators
- Secondary: Franchise development directors
- Tertiary: Press and industry influencers

## Delivery Model

### Experience Flow
1. Pre-event: Reserve War Room session landing page
2. At-event: 15-minute immersive experience with specialist
3. Post-event: Personalized market analysis follow-up
4. Online version: Virtual war room for remote attendees

### Key Interactions
- Large touchscreen showing local competitive landscape
- "Find Your Competitors" → enter location, see live map
- Takeaway: Printed "threat assessment" + trial code
- Photo opp: Branded backdrop with live data visualization

## Pitch Site Structure

**Hero**:
- Headline: "See your competitive landscape like never before."
- Visual: Panoramic photo of war room installation

**Sections**:
1. Video Walkthrough
2. Upcoming Events Schedule
3. Past Events Gallery
4. Private Demo Request
5. Virtual War Room

## Visual Design

**Hero Image Prompt**:
> "A dramatic wide-angle shot of a conference booth designed like a war room, with large LED screens showing restaurant pricing heat maps, operators gathered around, professional lighting, modern tech aesthetic."

## Implementation Notes
- Target: NRA Show, IFA Convention, regional franchise expos
- Rental A/V equipment costs ~$5-10K per event
- Requires trained on-site staff

## Strategic Value
- Justifies premium pricing through premium experience
- Generates social proof content (photos, testimonials)
- Creates urgency ("limited slots")
- High-intent lead generation from reservations
- Positions brand as enterprise-grade
