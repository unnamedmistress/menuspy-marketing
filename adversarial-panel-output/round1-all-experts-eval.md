## ROUND 1: EXPERT POSITIONS

### AI Engineer - Technical Feasibility Rankings

**TOP 5 (Easiest to Build & Scale):**
1. **CertAlert Pro (9/10)** - Structured government data, clear schemas, reliable APIs (where available). Can start with top 10 states, expand gradually. Low complexity, high value per subscriber.
2. **ReciprocityMap (9/10)** - Static reference data, one-time data collection effort, minimal ongoing scraping. Perfect for bootstrapped MVP with manual research then crowdsourced updates.
3. **DomainDetective (9/10)** - Domain drop lists are public, backlink APIs (Ahrefs/Moz) are mature. Entirely automatable, low infrastructure costs.
4. **ScholarSift (8/10)** - Fellowship websites are generally scraper-friendly, data is public, no ToS issues. RSS feeds common.
5. **ProfitScan (8/10)** + **SalaryScope (8/10)** (tie) - eBay API is robust, Facebook Marketplace scraping is technically challenging but doable with rotating proxies. H1B data is public DOL data.

**BOTTOM 5 (Most Complex):**
- CryptoClaw (5/10) - Requires sub-second latency infra, WebSocket connections to dozens of exchanges
- TenderIntel (5/10) - Fortune 1000 procurement pages are scattered, non-standard, heavily bot-protected
- TenderTrack (5/10) - Same challenge as TenderIntel but fewer targets
- InvoiceFactor (5/10) - Difficult to get reliable cash flow distress signals without privileged data
- FlipFinder (6/10) - Multi-source data merge (tax + water + code) with address standardization challenges

**Key Technical Insight:** Ideas requiring real-time scraping of well-protected corporate sites (TenderIntel, TenderTrack, CryptoClaw) face highest infra costs and maintenance burden.

### AI Theorist - Edge Durability Rankings

**TOP 5 (Most Durable Information Advantage):**
1. **CertAlert Pro (9/10)** - Regulatory data changes slowly, compliance deadlines are predictable. Users need ongoing alerts, creating recurring value. Low signal decay.
2. **ReciprocityMap (8/10)** - License rules change rarely. One accurate database has lasting value. Low maintenance, high accuracy.
3. **GrantRadar (7/10)** - Grant cycles are annual, but aggregation creates value through convenience not just timing. Moat is completeness, not speed.
4. **SalaryScope (7/10)** - H1B data is public but messy. User-contributed offer letters create proprietary dataset. Glassdoor effect but more specific.
5. **ScholarSift (6/10)** - Fellowship aggregation has value in completeness, though deadlines create natural churn/re-engagement.

**BOTTOM 5 (Fragile/Fleeting Edge):**
- CryptoClaw (2/10) - 60-second advantage decays instantly. Information becomes worthless after listing. Pure speed play, zero durability.
- BonusHunter (3/10) - Bank bonuses change frequently, users churn after optimizing. One-time use pattern.
- ProfitScan (4/10) - Arbitrage opportunities close quickly as more resellers act. eBay sold data is delayed by definition.
- LiquidationLens (4/10) - Liquidation pallets are first-come-first-served, inventory is unpredictable.
- DomainDetective (4/10) - Good domains get snatched instantly by drop-catching services with registry connections.

**Key Insight:** Durable value comes from ongoing compliance needs (CertAlert), reference data (ReciprocityMap), or network effects (SalaryScope). Fleeting arbitrage (CryptoClaw, BonusHunter) has low retention.

### Economist - Economic Potential Rankings

**TOP 5 (Highest Economic Value):**
1. **FlipFinder (9/10)** - Real estate wholesalers have HIGH willingness to pay. Single deal can net $10K-$50K. $149/mo is trivial if it surfaces one viable lead. TAM: 50K+ active wholesalers in US.
2. **DelinquentDollars (8/10)** - Tax lien investors buy hundreds of properties. Yield data justifies premium pricing. TAM: $10B+ tax lien market, thousands of active investors.
3. **TenderIntel (8/10)** - Enterprise sales reps have expense accounts. One captured RFP could be worth millions. $99/mo is negligible. TAM: 500K+ B2B sales reps.
4. **SalaryScope (8/10)** - Salary negotiation ROI is 10x annual subscription first year alone. $19/mo justified by potential $5K-$20K salary gains. TAM: 50M+ US workers.
5. **GrantRadar (7/10)** - Nonprofits pay for grant writers ($5K-$15K/month). $79/mo for lead gen is reasonable. TAM: 1.5M nonprofits in US.

**BOTTOM 5 (Weakest Economics):**
- ScholarSift (5/10) - Students are price-sensitive. $9/mo is high for broke grad students. Scholarship market fragmented.
- ReciprocityMap (5/10) - One-time $39 is low LTV. Traveling professionals are niche market.
- DomainDetective (5/10) - Domainers are cheap. $19/mo competes with free tools. Low retention.
- SurplusScout (5/10) - Government surplus buyers are bargain hunters by definition. Price sensitivity high.
- BonusHunter (5/10) - Competing with free blogs/subreddits. Affiliate revenue model is crowded.

**Key Insight:** Ideas serving audiences with clear ROI (real estate, sales, salary negotiation) can command premium pricing. Ideas serving students, bargain hunters face pricing pressure.

### Strategist - Defensibility Rankings

**TOP 5 (Strongest Moats):**
1. **CertAlert Pro (8/10)** - Data accuracy creates trust barrier. First-mover in specific license types builds SEO dominance. Network effects as professionals share tool.
2. **SalaryScope (7/10)** - User-contributed data creates virtuous cycle (more users = better data = more users). Glassdoor proved this model.
3. **ReciprocityMap (7/10)** - Once accurate database built, switching costs are convenience+habit. Reference data has natural stickiness.
4. **GrantRadar (6/10)** - 2,000+ source aggregation requires ongoing effort. Moat is operational (keeping up with portal changes), not technical.
5. **ScholarSift (5/10)** + **SurplusScout (5/10)** + **PatentFlip (5/10)** (tie) - Aggregation plays with moderate operational moats from data completeness.

**BOTTOM 5 (Easily Copied):**
- BonusHunter (2/10) - Simple price comparison, easily replicated. No proprietary data.
- CryptoClaw (2/10) - Speed war, no moat except infrastructure. Bigger players can outspend on latency.
- TenderIntel (4/10) - Scraping play with no data exclusivity. Other vendors can scrape same sources.
- LiquidationLens (3/10) - Monitoring public liquidation sites is trivial to copy.
- ProfitScan (3/10) - eBay API access is democratic. Facebook scraping is commodity tech.

**Key Insight:** Sustainable moats come from: user-contributed data (SalaryScope), trust/accuracy (CertAlert), or operational complexity at scale (GrantRadar). Simple scraping plays are indefensible.

---

### Regulator - Risk Assessment Rankings

**SAFEST 5 (Lowest Legal Risk):**
1. **CertAlert Pro (9/10)** - Government regulatory data is public domain. No scraping of private sites. Explicit public interest value.
2. **ReciprocityMap (9/10)** - Published government rules, no ToS violations. Static reference material.
3. **ScholarSift (9/10)** - Fellowship information is public, educational purpose. Generally scraper-friendly.
4. **SurplusScout (9/10)** - Government surplus data is explicitly public. GovAuctions.org model is established.
5. **GrantRadar (8/10)** - Foundations generally want publicity for grants. Low privacy concerns.

**RISKIEST 5 (Highest Legal Concerns):**
- CryptoClaw (3/10) - Potential frontrunning/market manipulation concerns. Exchange ToS violations. SEC scrutiny possible.
- TenderIntel (4/10) - Scraping Fortune 1000 procurement pages violates ToS. Corporate anti-bot measures. Cease & desist risk.
- TenderTrack (5/10) - Similar to TenderIntel, scraping private company websites.
- EvictionEdge (5/10) - Aggregating distress signals could trigger FDCPA, FCRA concerns if selling to debt collectors.
- InvoiceFactor (5/10) - Signaling cash flow distress could expose to insider trading, defamation adjacent issues.

**MODERATE RISK (6-7/10):**
- ProfitScan: eBay API is allowed, Facebook scraping violates ToS
- SalaryScope: H1B data is public, but offer letter collection has privacy considerations
- FlipFinder: Public records aggregation is legal but data accuracy liability exists

### Ethicist - Ethical Standing Rankings

**MOST ETHICAL 5 (Highest Social Value):**
1. **GrantRadar (9/10)** - Helps nonprofits find funding = social good. Enables mission-driven organizations. Unequivocally positive.
2. **CertAlert Pro (9/10)** - Helps professionals maintain credentials. Protects public through compliance. Prevents inadvertent lapses.
3. **ScholarSift (8/10)** - Helps students fund education. Democratizes access to opportunity. Reduces debt burden.
4. **SalaryScope (8/10)** - Promotes pay transparency. Empowers workers in negotiations. Reduces information asymmetry with employers.
5. **ReciprocityMap (8/10)** - Helps traveling professionals work across states. Enables labor mobility. Practical public service.

**MOST PROBLEMATIC 5 (Ethical Concerns):**
- EvictionEdge (4/10) - Identifying distressed landlords for predatory acquisition. Could enable exploitation.
- InvoiceFactor (4/10) - Signaling desperate businesses to factoring companies feels predatory. Financial distress profiteering.
- CryptoClaw (4/10) - Frontrunning retail investors. Extracts value from uninformed participants.
- PatentFlip (5/10) - Opportunistic filing on abandoned patents blocks others' legitimate use. Patent troll adjacent.
- BonusHunter (5/10) - Extracting bank bonuses without genuine customer relationships. Terms-of-service engineering.
- FlipFinder (5/10) - Profiting from others' tax distress. Can enable predatory wholesaling practices.

**NEUTRAL (6-7/10):**
- ProfitScan, LiquidationLens, RepoRush, SurplusScout - Market efficiency plays with mixed effects
- TenderIntel, TenderTrack - Legitimate business intelligence

---

### Futurist - Long-Term Viability Rankings

**MOST FUTURE-PROOF 5:**
1. **CertAlert Pro (8/10)** - Regulatory compliance isn't going away. Automation of deadline tracking adds value. AI won't eliminate this need.
2. **ReciprocityMap (7/10)** - License portability won't become obsolete. Reference data has enduring value. Minimal AI disruption risk.
3. **GrantRadar (7/10)** - Grant funding cycles are institutional. AI might help aggregation but won't eliminate the service.
4. **DelinquentDollars (6/10)** - Tax liens are enduring market. Yield optimization always valuable. Regulation may increase, not eliminate.
5. **ScholarSift (6/10)** + **SalaryScope (6/10)** + **SurplusScout (6/10)** (tie) - Core needs persist, though AI may commoditize some aggregation.

**MOST VULNERABLE TO DISRUPTION:**
- CryptoClaw (2/10) - AI will accelerate information propagation to near-zero latency. 60-second edge won't last. Exchanges improving own notification systems.
- BonusHunter (3/10) - AI agents will optimize credit card bonuses automatically. One-time optimization tool has no future.
- ProfitScan (4/10) - Computer vision + AI will make arbitrage detection commodity. Resale markets becoming more efficient.
- DomainDetective (4/10) - AI content generation reduces value of aged domains. Drop-catching competition intensifying.
- LiquidationLens (4/10) - Liquidation platforms themselves becoming more sophisticated, direct-to-consumer.

**Key Insight:** Ideas serving compliance deadlines and institutional workflows (CertAlert, ReciprocityMap) are most durable. Pure arbitrage/speed plays (CryptoClaw, BonusHunter) face AI obsolescence.

## CONSENSUS OBSERVATIONS (Round 1)

### Ideas with BROAD EXPERT SUPPORT (High Scores Across Categories):
1. **CertAlert Pro** - Scored 8-9 from nearly every expert. Consensus winner on technical feasibility, edge durability, ethics, and low risk.
2. **ReciprocityMap** - Strong scores on technical ease, edge durability, and low risk. Pricing concerns from Economist.
3. **GrantRadar** - Strong ethical scores, good durability, moderate technical complexity.
4. **SalaryScope** - Strong economic case, good defensibility via network effects, positive ethics.

### Ideas with EXPERT DISSENSUS (Mixed Scores):
1. **FlipFinder** - Economist loves it (9/10 economics), but Ethicist (5/10) and Strategist (5/10) concerned about predatory nature and defensibility.
2. **CryptoClaw** - Potential for revenue, but universally panned by Theorist (2/10), Futurist (2/10), Regulator (3/10) - high risk, no durability.
3. **TenderIntel** - Economist and Strategist see value, technical experts concerned about scraping complexity and legal risk.

### Ideas with WEAK CROSS-EXPERT SUPPORT:
1. **BonusHunter** - Low scores on durability (3/10), moat (2/10), longevity (3/10). Economically marginal.
2. **EvictionEdge** - Ethical concerns (4/10) and regulatory risk drag down otherwise viable concept.
3. **InvoiceFactor** - Ethical concerns (4/10), high legal risk (5/10), weak durability (4/10).
