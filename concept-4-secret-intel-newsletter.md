# Concept 4: The "Secret Menu Intelligence" Newsletter

## Overview
A **premium newsletter tier** with "early warning" intelligence: upcoming price changes, new menu items being tested, promotional rollouts.

## Problem Insight
The planned "Restaurant Pricing Pulse" is valuable but generic. Operators crave **insider knowledge** — what's coming before it hits mainstream.

## Core Solution
Tiered newsletter with escalating exclusivity and timeliness.

## Target Users
- Primary: Paying customers (retention/engagement)
- Secondary: Newsletter subscribers who upgrade
- Tertiary: Industry journalists seeking scoops

## Delivery Model

### Tier Structure
| Tier | Name | Content | Timing |
|------|------|---------|--------|
| Free | Pricing Pulse | Weekly summary | 48-hour delayed |
| Premium | Secret Intel | Real-time alerts | Immediate |
| Exclusive | Forecast | Quarterly strategy | Analyst calls |

### Content Types
- 🚨 **Red Alert**: Major competitor price drops
- 🔮 **Forecast**: Predicted promotional windows
- 🕵️ **Discovery**: New menu items being tested
- 📊 **Deep Dive**: Monthly category analysis
