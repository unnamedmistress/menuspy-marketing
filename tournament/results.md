# Team Architecture Tournament Results

## Overview
Five team architectures competed to solve unique creative challenges. Each team built a pitch website showcasing their solution. Below are the results and evaluations.

## Challenges
1. **Dynamic Role Rotation**: Reimagine Urban Transportation
2. **Antagonistic Pods**: Design a New Way to Reduce Food Waste
3. **Emergent Swarm**: Create a Mental Health App for Teens
4. **Improvisation Theater**: Build a Decentralized Music Platform
5. **Parallel Universe**: Invent a New Kind of Social Network

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Creative Originality**: How unique and innovative the solution is.
- **Technical Feasibility**: Practicality and implementation potential.
- **Visual/Design Quality**: Aesthetic appeal and usability of the pitch site.
- **Narrative Coherence**: Clarity and logical flow of the solution.
- **Speed/Efficiency**: How quickly and effectively the team delivered.

## Winner
**Parallel Universe** emerged as the winner due to its high scores across all criteria, particularly in creative originality and technical feasibility. The multi-verse approach to social networking demonstrated exceptional innovation and practicality.

## Conclusion
Each architecture brought unique strengths to the table, but Parallel Universe's ability to balance creativity with feasibility set it apart. This tournament highlights the potential of diverse team structures in solving complex problems.
